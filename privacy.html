Privacy

RoomTone for iPhone and iPad · October 2026

In short

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no RoomTone account.
  • RoomTone doesn't keep what your child listens to, searches for or says.
  • Settings, PIN and history stay on the device.
  • RoomTone talks to the speakers over your Wi-Fi only. There's no RoomTone server and no Sonos account in the app.
  • Only the search in Apple Music or Spotify, if you turn it on, goes to the internet.

Data on the device

RoomTone keeps on the iPhone or iPad: the allowed speakers, volume limits, bedtime and kiosk settings, the parental PIN (only as a derived check value, never in plain text), recently played items and, if you turned on the Spotify search, the sign-in Spotify's service for Sonos handed out. PIN and settings are kept in the device's keychain and aren't synced through iCloud. None of it is sent to RoomTone. Deleting and reinstalling the app starts over.

Voice input

The microphone is on only while your child speaks after tapping the microphone. Apple's speech recognition understands it on the device. The recording is neither kept nor sent anywhere; the recognized text is only used for the search.

Local network

RoomTone finds your Sonos speakers on the Wi-Fi and controls them directly over it. Commands and live updates stay on your home network.

Music search

If you turn on the search in Apple Music, what your child searches for goes to Apple (MusicKit). If you turn on the search in Spotify, it goes to Spotify's service for Sonos, which Sonos runs, together with the Spotify sign-in you gave there. RoomTone doesn't keep the searches; the services' own privacy statements apply.

Sonos

RoomTone needs no Sonos account. For the Spotify search, you sign in to Spotify once, on the page the Sonos app uses as well. What the speakers themselves process, such as when they play from music services, is covered by Sonos's privacy statement.

Children

Parents set RoomTone up, and children use it. RoomTone collects no data from children, has no accounts and shows no ads. Search words only go to the music services you turn on, to answer the search.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection, and the right to complain to a data protection authority. As RoomTone keeps no personal data for longer, there's usually nothing to disclose. Feel free to write with any question.

RoomTone is compatible with Sonos, but it is not a Sonos product and is not certified by Sonos. Sonos is a trademark of Sonos, Inc.
